# Privacy Policy

This page is built to keep your data on your device. This policy explains exactly what it does and
does not do with your data.

## The short version

The page collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking, no ads,
no cookies and no third-party scripts. Nothing is ever sent to the developer or to any NIU backend.

## What is stored in the browser

The page puts three values into this device's localStorage: the chosen appearance (dark or light)
and the last selection in the filter, that is model and region. They stay on the device and are
never transmitted. Clearing the browser data removes them.

The language is not stored, the page always starts in German.

## The only network connection the page makes

When you open or reload the page, your browser fetches the static files from the host, that is
`index.html`, `app.js`, `i18n.js`, `styles.css` and the icons. The host sees the normal access logs
every website has: your IP address and which file you requested. Nothing more. After that the page
calls no other service and sends nothing.

## What you fetch yourself

The links on this page point at third parties, and only your click establishes the connection:

- The APK is hosted on `files.catbox.moe`. When you download it, that provider sees your IP address.
- The `update.json` files and the firmware binaries are hosted on GitHub. When they are fetched,
  GitHub sees your IP address, both when you click the links here and when the app on your phone
  loads the files.

## No developer or NIU backend

There is no cloud account and no server operated by this project that receives your data. For
comparison: the original NIU app signs you in and talks extensively to a backend (account, GPS, ride
data). This page does none of that.
